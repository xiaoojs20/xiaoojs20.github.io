Posts and notes here appear on no fixed schedule. If you would rather not keep checking back, add the site to an RSS reader and new pieces will find you instead.

## What RSS is

RSS is a plain-text list of recent updates. The site writes each item's title, link, publication date and summary into a single file, and a reader fetches that file periodically to see whether anything new has appeared. No account is required, no email address changes hands, and the subscription exists only between you and your reader.

## How to subscribe

1. Pick a reader and sign up, or install a desktop or mobile app.
2. Find "Add subscription" and choose to add it by URL.
3. Paste one of the addresses above and confirm.

Some browser extensions detect the feed automatically, in which case the icon next to the address bar will work as well.

### Readers worth trying

- **Follow**: a newer reader that also pulls in sources like WeChat public accounts and X.
- **Feedly**: long-running service with web and mobile apps; the free tier is enough for casual reading.
- **Inoreader**: powerful rules and filtering.
- **NetNewsWire**: open-source client for macOS and iOS, free.
- **Reeder**: a paid client for Apple platforms with a carefully built interface.

## About email subscriptions

There is no email subscription at the moment. The site is statically hosted, has no backend to store addresses, and I would rather not add third-party tracking scripts just to run a form. If that changes, it will be noted here.
