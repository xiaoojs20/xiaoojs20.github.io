## Hi, I'm xiaoojs20 {#hi-im-xiaoojs20}

My name is Jinsong Xiao. I was born in 2002 and am from Jinjiang, Quanzhou, Fujian, China. Welcome to my personal space.

I study electrical engineering at Tsinghua University and minored in software engineering as an undergraduate. I’m now pursuing a master’s degree and expect to graduate in 2027; I currently have no plans to pursue a PhD. I hope to work on AI, large language models, and internet algorithms. I enjoy exploring across fields and turning ideas into things that are useful in everyday life. 💡

I have an easygoing personality and would love to meet more people and exchange ideas. 🙂

## Why I made this website

This is where I share what I learn, the projects I work on, and moments from daily life: sometimes a carefully written note, sometimes a photograph, and sometimes an idea that is still taking shape.

I made this website in part to help visitors get to know me and find things we might enjoy talking about. It is also a space for myself: as my understanding evolves, I hope that writing things down and looking back will help me make sense of my ideas, understand myself better, and see how I change over time. 🌱

## A little more about me {#a-little-more-about-me}

My MBTI type is ISTJ. Each letter describes a preference in how I approach things:

- **I · Introversion**: I like thinking independently and value meaningful conversations as well as personal space.
- **S · Sensing**: I pay attention to concrete facts and real experiences, and enjoy learning through practice.
- **T · Thinking**: When a problem comes up, I tend to analyze it and look for solutions.
- **J · Judging**: I like planning ahead and having some structure in work and life.

It is one way to reflect on myself, not a complete description of who I am.

I’m learning to make room for more than work and goals: rest, exercise, travel, and time with the people close to me matter too. When a problem comes up, I want to become better not only at finding solutions, but also at listening patiently and understanding how others feel.

## Work and interests {#work-and-interests}

### Work and exploration

I’m interested in large language models (LLMs), AI applications, search, ads and recommendation algorithms, and data analysis. I also make extensive use of AI agent tools and keep trying different ways of working; lately, my use has gradually converged on ChatGPT and Codex. 💻

### Everyday interests

In spare moments, I enjoy technology and investing podcasts and books, interesting videos, and strength training. I started taking fitness seriously in October 2026, with the goal of building a lean physique, and have also tried making a tool to track my workouts. 💪

My favorite anime are Dragon Ball and KochiKame.

I also enjoy playing Honor of Kings. I’ve been playing since eighth grade, all the way through to the third year of my master’s degree—about eleven years now. I mainly play jungle, but also play clash lane and marksman. When I’m not busy, I reach Grandmaster in pretty much every season. 🎮

### Travel and photography

On longer breaks, I like traveling to experience new places, photographing landscapes, and going cycling with friends. I hope to learn more about the world on my travels, rather than simply come home with photos, and I’m curious about the history, culture, and everyday life of the places I visit. 🌍 Portrait photography is not quite my strong suit yet, but I’m happy to keep learning. 📷

For photography, I use a Sony α6500 with E PZ 16–50mm F3.5–5.6 OSS and E 55–210mm F4.5–6.3 OSS lenses. For video, I use a DJI Osmo Pocket 3 to capture moments from my travels and everyday life.

Among the places I’ve visited, Tokyo and Ili stand out as favorites: one for its cityscape, the other for its natural scenery. I also made a [travel map]({{< relref "page/travel" >}}) to gradually collect the places I’ve been and the stories along the way.

## Life and what’s ahead {#life-and-whats-ahead}

### Lifestyle

I prefer mild food, don’t eat spicy dishes, and neither smoke nor drink alcohol. And I can’t go a day without coffee. ☕

### Some personal thoughts {#future-and-financial-plans}

Since 2025, I’ve been thinking more about investing, financial freedom and exploring the world. Looking ahead, I hope to visit more countries, do well with investing in US stocks, and build a healthy, strong body. I value steady progress over time and hope that work, learning, and thoughtful planning will give me more choices in life. For now, I don’t plan to buy a home or a car before turning thirty; if I settle in China long term, I’d most likely choose Beijing or Shanghai, with a current preference for Shanghai. I value practical purchases over keeping up appearances, while being happy to spend on travel, experiences, and quality of life.

I hope this website can be a space that grows gradually over time, and a way to meet interesting people. 🙂
