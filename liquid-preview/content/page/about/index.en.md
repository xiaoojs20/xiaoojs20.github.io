## Hi, I'm xiaoojs20

My name is Jinsong Xiao. I was born in 2002, am 175 cm tall, and am from Jinjiang, Quanzhou, Fujian, China. Welcome to my personal space.

I grew up in rural Jinjiang and later came to Tsinghua University to study electrical engineering, with a minor in software engineering as an undergraduate. I’m now pursuing a master’s degree and expect to graduate in 2027; I currently have no plans to pursue a PhD. I hope to work on AI, large language models, and internet algorithms. I enjoy exploring across fields and turning ideas into things that are useful in everyday life.

I’m an ISTJ with an easygoing personality. I’d love to meet more people and exchange ideas. 🙂

This is where I share what I learn, the projects I work on, and moments from daily life: sometimes a carefully written note, sometimes a photograph, and sometimes an idea that is still taking shape.

I made this website in part to help visitors get to know me and find things we might enjoy talking about. It is also a space for myself: as my understanding evolves, I hope that writing things down and looking back will help me make sense of my ideas, understand myself better, and see how I change over time.

## A little more about me

I tend to think things through independently and plan ahead, and I enjoy learning and trying new things. I value meaningful conversations, while also appreciating the time and space we each need for ourselves.

I’m learning to make room for more than work and goals: rest, exercise, travel, and time with the people close to me matter too. When a problem comes up, I want to become better not only at finding solutions, but also at listening patiently and understanding how others feel.

## Work and interests

I’m interested in large language models (LLMs), AI applications, search, advertising and recommendation algorithms, and data analysis. In spare moments, I enjoy technology and investing podcasts and books, interesting videos, and strength training. I started taking fitness seriously in October 2026, with the goal of building a lean physique, and have also tried making a tool to track my workouts.

On longer breaks, I like traveling to experience new places, photographing landscapes, and going cycling with friends. I hope to learn more about the world on my travels, rather than simply come home with photos, and I’m curious about the history, culture, and everyday life of the places I visit. Portrait photography is not quite my strong suit yet, but I’m happy to keep learning.

## Life and what’s ahead

I prefer mild food, don’t eat spicy dishes, and neither smoke nor drink alcohol. I’m usually fairly busy, though I’ve had more free time in recent months and would be glad to chat.

Since 2025, I’ve been thinking more about investing, financial freedom and exploring the world. Looking ahead, I hope to visit more countries, do well with investing in US stocks, and build a healthy, strong body. I value steady progress over time and hope that work, learning, and thoughtful planning will give me more choices in life. For now, I don’t plan to buy a home or a car before turning thirty; if I settle in China long term, I’d most likely choose Beijing or Shanghai, with a current preference for Shanghai. I value practical purchases over keeping up appearances, while being happy to spend on travel, experiences, and quality of life.

## Relationships and family

I take relationships seriously and hope for one built around a long-term life together. Honesty, loyalty, trust, and communication matter to me. I hope we can support and spend time with each other while each keeping our own interests, careers, and personal space. When we disagree, I hope we can listen to each other’s feelings and work things out together, rather than simply trying to prove who is right.

I value my family and hope to take responsibility for caring for my parents. In a family of my own, I hope both partners can discuss things as equals and make decisions together: where to live, how to manage finances, and how our families are involved should all be considered with respect for each other’s views. I value saving and long-term investing, while recognizing a family’s need for stability. Whether and when to buy a home should depend on our actual needs and circumstances, and be decided together.

I feel positive about marriage and the possibility of having children, while recognizing that the details should reflect both partners’ wishes and practical circumstances. I prefer a simple, meaningful wedding. My idea of a good life is one in which we each have work and interests we enjoy, and can also exercise, travel, try new things, and share comfortable everyday moments together.

I hope this website can be a space that grows gradually over time, and a way to meet interesting people. 🙂
